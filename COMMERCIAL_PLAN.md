# Fixed-price pilot

Target platform engineering teams maintaining large Rust or mixed-language monorepos with a custom CI artifact filter, release packager, or watcher. Qualify a buyer who owns CI cost or build tooling and can provide a scrubbed path/rule corpus, a baseline, and a rollback owner. Avoid teams whose primary need is full Git traversal.

Offer: **$1,000 fixed price** for one repository and one workflow. Deliver a baseline profile, a semantics and latency report against the existing matcher, a shadow-mode integration and rollback instructions. Agree on an end-to-end latency target before integration; require zero unexplained decisions on the agreed corpus before recommending rollout. No production deployment or private-data transfer without separate approval.

Start a 14-day clock at first outreach to a qualified buyer. Approach 10 qualified teams; ask a named budget owner for a purchase order or signed $1,000 pilot. If none arrives by day 14, kill this offer and stop outreach and paid development pending a new hypothesis. Meetings, compliments and unpaid trials do not count. No outreach or orders have been booked in Wave 1.

Upstream general fixes, portability work, and anonymized reproducible tests to this Apache-2.0 project or the relevant incumbent under its contribution rules. Seek upstream merge before maintaining a fork; document rejection or an unresolved review and keep any temporary fork narrow. Never merge private fixtures, secrets, or customer integration code without explicit permission. Charge for integration, validation and support, not access to fixes.
